Questions, answered plainly.

Is Revolut a bank?

In most markets Revolut operates under an e-money or banking licence depending on region, and eligible balances are safeguarded or deposit-protected accordingly. The specifics are shown in-app before you open an account.

Which currencies can I hold?

You can hold and exchange between 30+ currencies inside the app, and spend in over 150 with a card that auto-converts at the point of sale.

How does the real exchange rate work?

Standard and Plus get a fee-free exchange allowance each month at the interbank rate; Premium, Metal and Ultra get it unlimited. Past the allowance, a small fair-usage fee applies.

Can I trade crypto and stocks from the same balance?

Yes — crypto and fractional stock trading sit in the same app and the same balance, so moving money between spending, saving and investing takes one tap, not a transfer to a separate broker.
