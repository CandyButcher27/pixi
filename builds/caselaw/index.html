What a matter holds

One chronology, from instruction to archive.

  1. CorrespondenceEmail threaded into the matter on arrival, with the attachment versioned, not duplicated.
  2. Time and disbursementsRecorded against the file as you work, not reconstructed on the last Friday of the month.
  3. Court and limitation datesEntered once, watched thereafter, with the responsible fee earner named.
  4. Closing and retentionArchive on a retention clock that matches your regulator, not a folder someone forgot.
