HALL A — THE RELAY ROOM

Rebuilt around a 34-cabinet horn-loaded soundsystem salvaged from the site's original signal-transfer hall. Runs low, runs loud, runs mono until 2am. Capacity 900. No visuals beyond the room's own bare copper wiring, left exposed on purpose.

HALL B — THE COLD ROOM

A smaller, sub-zero former refrigeration bay converted into a quadraphonic listening room — four independent speaker arrays, four independent mixes, one shared floor. Capacity 300. Coats provided at the door; nothing else is.

YARD — BETWEEN THE TWO

Open-air concrete yard connecting both halls. No stage, no sound of its own — just somewhere to stand between transmissions and hear both rooms bleed into each other.
