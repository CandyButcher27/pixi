Process

"We roast to reveal the farm, not the roaster."

How we roast

01

Select

Each lot is cupped blind against the last three harvests before we commit to a bag. Half of what we sample never makes the list.

02

Roast

Twelve kilos at a time, on a 1950s drum rebuilt in Marvila. Every batch is logged by temperature, not by the clock.

03

Rest

Beans rest a minimum of five days before sale — long enough for the CO₂ to settle, never long enough to go stale on the shelf.

04

Grind & cup

Ground to order at the counter, or left whole for the bag. We cup a spoonful of every batch before it goes out.
