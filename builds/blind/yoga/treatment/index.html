Four practices

What happens on the mat

  1. Hatha

    Held postures and plain breathing. The room for learning what your hips actually do, at 06:45 and noon.

    9 classes

  2. Vinyasa

    Movement linked to breath, taught steady rather than fast. Sequences change monthly and are posted by the door.

    8 classes

  3. Yin

    Long, quiet holds on the floor, three to five minutes each. Blankets are warmed on the radiator before class.

    6 classes

  4. Restorative

    Bolsters, eye pillows, very little effort. Built for the end of a long week and for anyone coming back from injury.

    5 classes

Who teaches

Four teachers, each with at least five hundred hours of training and a regular slot, so you can follow one voice through the week.
