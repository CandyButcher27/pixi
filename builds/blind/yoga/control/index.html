Today at Stillwater

    Four ways
    to practise

    Every class is 60 or 75 minutes, taught by a single teacher, with time to rest at the end. Props are provided. Come as you are.

    i.

    Slow Flow

    Vinyasa at half speed. Linked breath and movement, with room to feel each shape.

    All levels · 60 min
    ii.

    Yin & Restore

    Long, supported holds on bolsters and blocks. The class people book for Sunday evening.

    All levels · 75 min
    iii.

    Strong Hatha

    Standing work, balances and inversions, built carefully over a six-week arc.

    Intermediate · 75 min
    iv.

    Breath & Sit

    Pranayama and twenty quiet minutes of meditation. No experience needed.

    All levels · 45 min
