From quarry to kiln

Seven months per firing cycle
01

Dig

Local clay from a disused china-clay pit, blended with a bought stoneware body for strength.

02

Throw

Forms start on a kick wheel, then are trimmed, coiled or faceted at leather-hard.

03

Rest

Weeks of slow drying under cloth in the cold room. Rushing it cracks the foot.

04

Fire

Thirty hours of stoking to 1300°C. Ash settles and melts into glaze where the flame passes.
