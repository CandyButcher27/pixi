Twice a year, eight friends and I stoke the kiln around the clock for three days. Ash from the pine rides the flame through the chamber and melts onto the shoulders of the pots, so no two leave the same. Below is the log from firing no. 41.

Trained in Mashiko, working in Visby.

I spent four years apprenticed at a family pottery in Tochigi before coming home to set up a studio in a converted limestone barn. I dig a third of my clay from the island's marl beds and blend it with a Westerwald stoneware for strength.
