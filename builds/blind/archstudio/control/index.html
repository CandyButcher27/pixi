02 — Practice

We start with the section, not the facade.

Every project begins as a cut through the site: where the water table sits, where the sun falls at 15:00 in February, how far a child walks to the nearest window. Material comes after — usually brick, often reclaimed, always local.

  1. iDaylight first

    Floor plates no deeper than 12 m, so no desk is far from a window.

  2. iiReuse before build

    Over half our work since 2019 has kept an existing structure standing.

  3. iiiBuilt to weather

    Details drawn for salt air, rain sideways and a hundred winters.
