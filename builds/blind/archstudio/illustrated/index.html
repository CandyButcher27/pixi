02 — Method

We start with a pencil and end with a site visit.

i. Walk

Walk the site

Every project begins with a day on foot: light, wind, where people already sit.

ii. Section

Draw the section

We fix the cut through the building first. Plans follow from where light can reach.

iii. Model

Build it in card

Hand-cut models at 1:200, photographed in real daylight on the studio roof.

iv. Return

Go back after a year

We revisit every building after its first winter and publish what we got wrong.
