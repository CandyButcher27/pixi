Every bowl starts from the same broth. What changes is the tare, the oil and the cut of the noodle.

Noodle firmness: say katame (firm), futsuu (normal) or yawame (soft) when you hand over your ticket. Halal and vegetarian broths are not available.

Broth by the hour

Eighteen hours, one pot.

The 120-litre stockpot is never empty. Each day's broth is built on the bones of yesterday's, so the flavour you taste tonight started on Thursday. This is the schedule the kitchen keeps.

  1. 05:00Bones in32 kg of pork trotters and back bones, blanched and scrubbed, into the pot with yesterday's base.
  2. 07:30Hard boilRolling boil to emulsify the fat. The kitchen fan goes to full; the neighbours know the smell.
  3. 09:40Noodles cutHokkaido flour, 34% hydration, kansui from Mongolia. Rested in boxes until service.
  4. 11:00Niboshi in2.4 kg of Seto Inland Sea sardines, heads and guts removed by hand, steeped off the heat.
  5. 11:30Lunch serviceFirst bowls out. The broth is lighter at noon and thicker by evening — regulars choose their hour.
  6. 15:00BreakDoors close, pot topped with fresh bones. Chashu rolled and tied for tomorrow.
  7. 17:30Dinner serviceStrongest broth of the day. Yuzu-shio on Saturdays only.
  8. 23:30Strain & restA third of the broth is kept back to start the next pot at 05:00.

House rules · カウンターの約束

How the counter works.

  1. Buy a ticket before you queue.The machine is left of the door, English buttons on the bottom row. Cash, ICOCA and Suica. No cards.
  2. Hand the ticket over when you sit.Tell us your noodle firmness and whether you want extra oil (abura mashi). That is the only choice you make.
  3. Eat within twenty minutes, if you can.Not a rule, a courtesy. There are people outside. Slurp freely — it cools the noodle.
  4. Groups sit where seats open.We cannot hold a row together. Parties of more than four, come before 18:00.
  5. Return your bowl to the ledge.Wipe the counter with the cloth in front of you. Say "gochisousama" if you like — the kitchen will answer.
  6. No photos of the kitchen staff.Bowls are fine. Flash is not.
  7. Pick your seat if you get the choice.Seats 1–6 face the stockpot. Seat 11 is by the door and gets the draft in winter. Prams fold under seat 10.

First time

Ticket machine in four steps

  1. Insert cash or tap ICOCA / Suica.
  2. Press a ramen button — top row is the house bowl.
  3. Add toppings from the yellow buttons.
  4. Take your tickets and change, then join the queue.
