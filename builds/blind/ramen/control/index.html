8Seats at one cedar counter

No tables, no reservations, no menu to read aloud. You buy a ticket at the machine, hand it over the counter, and Tetsu-san starts your bowl.

We cook one tonkotsu-gyokai broth in a 120-litre stockpot that hasn't been fully emptied since the shop opened. Noodles are cut every afternoon from Hokkaido flour, 22% hydration, thick and slightly wavy so they carry the soup. When the pot runs dry, we close — sometimes at 1am, sometimes at 3.
