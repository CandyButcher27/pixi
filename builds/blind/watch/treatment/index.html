The atelier

Eleven people.
Fourteen months.
One movement.

We do not run a production line. A Vauclair calibre is cut, bevelled, polished and regulated in a single room above the river, at the pace the steel allows.

  1. Plates cut from German silver
  2. Anglage by hand, 74 inner angles
  3. Côtes de Genève, one stroke per bridge
  4. Hairspring set in six positions
  5. Forty days on the regulation bench
