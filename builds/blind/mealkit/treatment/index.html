How it works

Sunday you pick.
Tuesday you cook.

  1. Choose 2–5 recipes

    From the twelve above. Swap, skip or pause until Sunday 23:59; after that the farms start harvesting for you.

    ~3 min

  2. Farms pick to order

    Monday morning, our seven farms harvest only what has been ordered. Average field-to-box time is 31 hours.

    31 h

  3. We portion every gram

    Spices are weighed to 0.1 g, sauces sealed in compostable sachets, each recipe bagged separately and labelled.

    0.1 g

  4. It arrives cold, you cook

    Paper-insulated box keeps under 5 °C for 48 hours. Recipe cards list prep, cook and wash-up times separately.

    < 5 °C

Before you order

Can I skip a week?

Yes, any week, up to Sunday 23:59. Skipped weeks cost nothing.

Is it fully vegan?

Every ingredient, including sauces, wine in the cassoulet and the sugar in the pickles. Certified by Vegan Action.

What about nut allergies?

Tree nuts and peanuts are packed on a separate line; recipes are tagged in the menu.

What do I do with the box?

Paper box and ice pack go in recycling; liners and sachets in compost.
