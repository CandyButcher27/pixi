Questions, answered

Still stuck? Write to hello@example.com.

Is everything 100% vegan?

Yes. Every ingredient, sauce and garnish is plant-based, and our kitchen handles no animal products at all.

Can I skip a week or cancel?

Skip, pause or cancel from your account any time before Thursday 11:59pm for the following Monday's delivery.

How do you handle allergies?

Each recipe lists the 14 major allergens. You can set allergen filters so those recipes never appear in your picks.

What happens to the packaging?

Liners and ice packs are home-compostable; boxes are recyclable cardboard. Return any box with your next delivery and we'll reuse it.

Do you deliver to my area?

We currently deliver to 41 cities across the western and central US. Enter your zip at checkout to confirm.
