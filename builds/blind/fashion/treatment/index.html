The ledger

Where the money in one jacket goes.

Every Fallow piece ships with its own ledger: who grew the fibre, who wove it, who sewed it, and what each of them was paid. This is the field jacket, line by line.

Our margin is the smallest line we can keep the lights on with. There is no line for discounting, because there is no discounting.

Bring it back.

A torn hem, a lost button, an elbow worn through: send it to us and we mend it, free, for as long as the piece exists. When you are finished with it, we take it back and it becomes next year's stuffing, rags or yarn.

  1. Request a label

    Tell us what's wrong. We email a prepaid label, no box needed.

  2. We mend it in Porto

    Our repair bench is in the same atelier that sewed it, so the thread matches.

  3. It comes home

    Usually inside three weeks, with a note of what was done added to its ledger.

One letter a year.

We write when the new season is ready, and not otherwise. No drops, no countdowns, no "last chance".
