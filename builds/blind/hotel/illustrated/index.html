Dar Nouaria · Medina, Marrakech

The street door is plain. Everything else is not.

Riads were built to face inward. From the derb you see a studded cedar door and a wall the colour of the city; step through and the noise of the souk drops away to water and birdsong under two bitter-orange trees.

We kept the tadelakt walls, the hand-cut zellige and the carved plaster that took a family of maâlems three years to repair. We added deep mattresses, proper showers and a roof that catches the evening call to prayer from four minarets at once.
