09Rooms, never more

A merchant's house, restored by the families who built it, one tile at a time.

Dar Zellij was a spice trader's home for four generations. In 2019 we spent three years with local maalems — master craftsmen — resetting hand-cut zellige, re-plastering walls in tadelakt, and carving cedar ceilings the way their grandfathers did.

There is no lobby. There is a fountain, a fig tree, the call to prayer at dusk, and someone who will bring you mint tea before you have asked.
