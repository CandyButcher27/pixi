01 · The machine

One motor. No gearbox.

The motor sits inside the swingarm pivot, so the chain line never changes as the suspension moves. That keeps the rear planted under hard drive, and it is why a Varro feels calm at the exit of a corner instead of twitchy.

There is no clutch and no gearbox to service. The only scheduled work is chain, brake pads and tyres. We publish every torque spec and wiring diagram, and any independent shop can do it.

02 · Charging

Plug in at home. Or carry it in.

Every Varro takes a standard Type 2 plug at up to 11 kW, and DC fast charging at 40 kW on the Strand and Coil. The Tern's 9.8 kWh pack lifts out in two 11 kg halves for flats without a garage.
