03 — The workshop

We spent fifty years building ship engines. Now we build the absence of one.

Built by hand

Every frame is TIG-welded in Gothenburg by a team of fourteen. Each bike carries the signature of the person who built it, under the seat.

Batteries that come home

When a pack drops below 80% capacity we take it back, rebuild it into grid storage, and credit you toward the next one.
