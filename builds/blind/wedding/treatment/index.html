04Questions, then your date

What couples ask first.

Will you tell us where to stand?

Twice: for family groups, and for about two minutes on your walk if the light asks for it. The rest of the day I follow, I don't direct.

How many photographs do we get?

Roughly 110 per hour of coverage, every one edited by me. I cut duplicates and closed eyes; I don't cut the uncle.

Why six weeks?

I edit every gallery myself, in order, one wedding at a time. Six weeks is the median over the last 40; you get 60 previews in 48 hours if you add them.

Do you shoot film?

A few rolls of Portra 400 and HP5 at every wedding, mixed into the gallery. Developed and scanned in Riga.

Can we have the raw files?

No — the edit is the work. You do get full‑resolution JPEGs and permission to print them anywhere.

Do you travel?

Yes, mostly by train. 18 countries so far. Travel is invoiced at cost; no day rate for travel days under six hours.

What if it rains?

Then the photographs have rain in them. Three of my favourite weddings did. I bring two clear umbrellas.

How do we hold a date?

A short call, a one‑page contract, and a €600 deposit. The balance is due two weeks before the wedding.
