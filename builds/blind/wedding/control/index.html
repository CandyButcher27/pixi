01 — Approach

We stand at the edge of the room and wait for the moments nobody planned.

Most of your wedding happens between the scheduled parts: a father fixing a cufflink, a friend laughing too hard at the wrong speech, the five minutes you finally get alone. That is where we work.

We shoot a mix of medium-format film and digital, take on twenty weddings a year, and deliver every gallery by hand — edited, sequenced and printed, not dumped into a folder.

02 — How it goes
  1. I

    A conversation

    An hour on a call or over coffee. We want to know who is in the room.

  2. II

    The plan

    Light, timings, the people who matter. You get one page, not a questionnaire.

  3. III

    The day

    Two of us, from getting ready to the last dance. Barely noticed.

  4. IV

    The book

    Gallery in six weeks; a linen-bound album, sequenced like a story.
