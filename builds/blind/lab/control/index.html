How do forests remember drought?

We build models of how ecosystems carry the memory of past stress — from root networks to satellite canopies — and test them in 40 field plots across three continents.

§1 Research
  1. 01

    Hydraulic legacy

    Measuring how xylem damage from one dry summer shapes growth for the next decade.

  2. 02

    Mycorrhizal signalling

    Tracing carbon and stress cues through fungal networks with isotope labelling.

  3. 03

    Canopy from orbit

    Fusing Sentinel-2 and lidar to detect stress weeks before visible dieback.

  4. 04

    Forecasting mortality

    Probabilistic models that turn plot data into regional risk maps for forest managers.
