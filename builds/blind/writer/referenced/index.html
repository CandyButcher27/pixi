Three
passes

Every draft goes through the same quiet routine an old editor would run — only faster, and only when you call for it.

01

Read

It reads the whole piece before touching a line — your argument, your rhythm, the words you lean on.

02

Mark

Notes land in the margin, never in your sentence. Each one says why, in a single plain line.

03

Hand back

You accept, reject or rewrite. Marginal learns your refusals and stops suggesting what you never take.
