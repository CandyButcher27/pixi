One paragraph, three kinds of help.

i

Feed it your past work

Point it at a folder of essays, a newsletter archive, or a Google Doc. It builds a private model of your rhythm, diction and habits.

ii

Write where you write

Works inside Docs, Notion, Gmail and plain text. No new editor to learn, no copy-paste into a chat box.

iii

Accept, reject, argue

Every suggestion explains itself. Reject one and it learns why. Push back and it will make its case — or concede.

House rules we wrote for the machine.

  1. Never invent a fact.If a claim needs a source, it flags the sentence. It does not fabricate one.
  2. Your sentences stay yours.Suggestions are capped at the clause level unless you ask for a full rewrite.
  3. Plain beats impressive.It will cut "leverage" before it adds "delve".
  4. Your drafts are not training data.Your voice model is encrypted, yours, and deleted when you leave.
