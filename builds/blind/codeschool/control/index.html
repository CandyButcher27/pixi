What a session looks like

90 minutes, once or twice a week. Snacks included, screens off for the last ten.

0:00

Warm-up puzzle

An unplugged logic game to get brains moving. Usually involves cups, string or a very confused teacher-robot.

0:15

One new idea

Ten minutes, never more. Today: what a loop actually is.

for step in range(4):
  robot.move("up")
0:25

Build time

Kids work on their own project with the new idea. Teachers roam, ask questions, and resist the urge to grab the mouse.

1:20

Show & tell

Everyone shows one thing that works and one bug they squashed. Bugs get applause too.

Questions parents ask

My kid has never coded. Is that okay?

Most of our students start from zero. The trial class includes a quick, no-pressure chat so we can place them in the right group.

Do they need their own computer?

No. Every studio has laptops and kits. If you'd like them to keep building at home, we'll tell you exactly what's worth buying (usually nothing).

Who are the teachers?

Working developers, engineering students and trained educators. All are background-checked and complete our teaching course before leading a class.

What if they miss a session?

Book a make-up at any studio within the same term, or watch the short recap video and catch up with a teacher at the next class.
