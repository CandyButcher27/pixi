04 — Repair, forever

Crash it. Send it back. We stitch it.

Every Échappée garment is repaired free for as long as it exists. Postage both ways is on us inside the EU; turnaround is ten working days.

  • ATears, abrasion and failed seams from crashes: free, no questions, no receipt.
  • BWorn chamois replaced at cost (€ 38), fitted to your existing bib.
  • CBeyond repair? We recycle the yarn and take 30 % off the replacement.
