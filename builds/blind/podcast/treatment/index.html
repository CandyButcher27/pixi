04
How a show gets made

Months, not minutes

We publish less than any network our size, on purpose. Every episode passes through the same four rooms before it reaches your feed.

  1. Reporting

    Hosts get a paid reporting window of up to twelve weeks before a single episode is scheduled.

  2. The edit room

    Two editors per episode, one who knows the story and one who has never heard of it.

  3. Fact check

    Every claim is sourced and every voice is called back before air.

  4. The mix

    Scored and mixed in-house, mastered for headphones and for the car.
