    03 / Story

    “We never made more than we could sell by lunch. That's the whole business plan.”

    — Ines Marlow, baker & owner

    Ines started baking from a borrowed kitchen on Mill Lane with one sack of local wheat and a starter she'd kept alive through three house moves. Fifteen years on, the starter is the same.

    Our flour comes from Fallow Farm, eleven miles north, stone-milled weekly. Butter is cultured, eggs are free-range, and anything left at close goes to the shelter on Corn Street.
