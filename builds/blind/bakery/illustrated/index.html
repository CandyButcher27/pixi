ii.

What happens before you wake up

  1. 4:00 AM

    Fire's lit

    Maple and oak. The oven takes ninety minutes to come up to 260°C.

  2. 5:30 AM

    Loaves in

    Shaped last night, proofed cold overnight, scored by hand. Forty at a time.

  3. 7:00 AM

    Doors open

    The first croissants are cooling. The regulars already know the bench.

  4. 11:00 AM

    Tomorrow begins

    Mixing starts for tomorrow's dough. Gordon the starter gets fed.

  5. 3:00 PM

    Second baguettes

    Then we sweep up and give what's left to the shelter on Arden Road.

“My mother opened this shop with one oven and a borrowed mixer. We have two ovens now, and we still borrow the mixer sometimes.”
— Ines Hollis, head baker, second generation
