How a loaf happens

Thirty-one hours for one loaf.

  1. Mon 9amLevain is fed with stone-ground wheat from a mill forty minutes away.
  2. Mon 4pmMixed, rested, folded by hand four times through the afternoon.
  3. OvernightShaped loaves sit in a cold room and slowly turn sour.
  4. Tue 4amScored and loaded into an oven fired with hornbeam since 2am.
  5. Tue 7amThe door opens. The first loaves are still crackling.

Flour, water, salt.
