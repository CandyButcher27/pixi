Custody

How the ratio is produced

  1. At 00:00 UTC we snapshot every client balance into a Merkle tree. Your leaf hash is in Account → Proofs.
  2. Wallet addresses sign a message containing that root. Anyone can verify the signatures against the chain.
  3. An independent auditor checks liabilities monthly; the last report (Oct 2026) is linked unredacted.
  4. 96% of assets sit in 3-of-5 multisig cold storage across three countries. Hot wallets are capped at 4% and topped up manually.
