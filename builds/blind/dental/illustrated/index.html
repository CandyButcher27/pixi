An hour at Willow Street, drawn out

Most of our patients say the worst part is not knowing what comes next. So here is the whole visit, left to right.

0 – 5 min

Arrive

No clipboard. Your notes are already open, and the kettle is on.

5 – 15 min

Talk

Sitting upright, face to face, before anyone reaches for a mirror.

15 – 50 min

Treat

Everything we see goes on the ceiling screen. Raise a hand to pause, any time.

50 – 60 min

Go home

A written plan, a new brush, and your next visit booked only if you want it.
