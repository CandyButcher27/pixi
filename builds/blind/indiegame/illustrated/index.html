Chapter one — the high water

A town under the tide, and one small light.

You are Wick, a moth who inherited a lighthouse nobody else wanted. Each night the water pulls a little more of Holloway down. Each night you row out to find what it left behind: a teacup, a letter, a neighbour who never learned to swim.

Lanternwake is a hand-drawn rowing and repair game about tending things that cannot be saved, only kept. There is no combat. There is weather, and there is the dark.

Three things to do each night

01

Row the flooded streets

Physics-light rowing that feels like a lullaby. Tides shift the map every night, so the shortcut to the chapel might be gone by Tuesday.

02

Mend what you bring home

Every keepsake is a small hand-built puzzle. Fix a music box and its song plays across the bay; fix a clock and the tide waits a minute longer.

03

Keep the light burning

Your beam does more than guide boats. Where it falls, Holloway briefly remembers itself, and the town that was shows you the way to what's left.
