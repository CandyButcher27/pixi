01 — The sea

An archipelago that drowns a little more each run.

Every voyage generates a new chain of islands from a single 32-bit seed, then floods it: the tide line rises one tile per in-game day, so the harbour you mapped on day one is a reef by day five. Five biomes stack from the warm Shelf down to the Black Trench, each with its own weather table, fish, and things that are not fish.

Share a seed with a friend and you sail the same sea — same wrecks, same storms, same lighthouse with one keeper who will lie to you about the currents.

02 — Systems

Your boat is the character sheet.

No skill trees. Every upgrade is a physical part bolted into a 9×5 hull grid — and every part has weight, so a bigger net sinks your waterline and slows you into the next squall.

  1. Plank-by-plank hull

    48 parts across sails, keels, nets, lanterns and stoves. Breaks are local: a holed bow floods the bow.

    Slots 1–45 · 48 parts

  2. Fish with memory

    Each species learns your last three lures. Use the same spinner all run and the Shelf goes quiet.

    61 species · 3-lure memory

  3. Lantern oil as a clock

    Oil is light, heat and score. Burn it to see the reef or sell it to buy rope — never both.

    1 tank · 6 burn rates

  4. Wrecks persist

    Your sunk boat stays on that seed with its cargo. The next captain can salvage it, or the gulls will.

    Per-seed · salvageable

  5. Weather you can read

    Barometer, cloud shape and gull behaviour telegraph storms 90 seconds ahead. Learn the sky, not a UI bar.

    90 s warning · 7 storm types
