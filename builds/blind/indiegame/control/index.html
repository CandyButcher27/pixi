Every dusk, the mountain rewrites itself.

You are Ivo, a lamplighter's apprentice, and your village has gone dark. The only flame left sits in a paper lantern that burns a little lower with every step.

Paths shift after sundown. Bridges you crossed yesterday lead somewhere new. The spirits who live in the fog remember things you have forgotten, if you are patient enough to listen.

Two of us made this over five years in a converted garage in Bergen. We hope it feels like a long walk with a good friend.
