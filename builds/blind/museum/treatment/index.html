Museum of modern & contemporary art · founded 1931 · in the grain silos since 2009

Everything in this building was once called not art.

Ostkai holds 6,412 works made after 1905, from the first abstract paintings shown in this city to video work acquired last spring. Four floors of silo, one rule: hang the argument, not the masterpiece.

The collection · 1905 to now

The collection in numbers

We publish the holdings every year, including what we do not show. 91% of the collection is in storage; any work can be requested for the study room with 10 days’ notice.

6,412Works held

Paintings, sculpture, film, works on paper, performance scores.

1,388Artists

From 61 countries; 37% women, up from 19% in 2010.

9%On view

578 works across 14 rooms this season.

€2.1mAcquisitions 2026

23 works bought, 41 gifted, 3 returned to source communities.

312Loans out
