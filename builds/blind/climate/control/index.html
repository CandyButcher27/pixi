01 / Demands

Three things we will not stop asking for.

i.

No new fossil fuel permits.

Not one more pipeline, terminal or drilling lease approved after this year. The infrastructure we build now locks in emissions for forty more.

ii.

Divest public money.

Teachers, nurses and firefighters should not be funding the fires. We want every public pension out of coal, oil and gas by 2030.

iii.

A just transition, in writing.

Guaranteed wages, retraining and pensions for every worker whose job changes. A plan that leaves people behind is not a plan.

"We didn't win the pension vote with a petition. We won it with three hundred people in a room that seats ninety."
Chapter organiser, Tacoma · 2024 divestment win
