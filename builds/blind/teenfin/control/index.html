04 / FAQ

Stuff people ask.

Is it actually free?

Yes. No monthly fee, no ATM fees in-network, no overdraft fees because you can't overdraft.

Is my money safe?

Funds are held at an FDIC-insured partner bank. Loot is a financial technology company, not a bank.

Do my parents see everything?

They see balances and can set limits. You choose whether they get a ping for every purchase.

What happens when I turn 18?

Your account graduates with you. Same card, your own name on everything, no parent required.
