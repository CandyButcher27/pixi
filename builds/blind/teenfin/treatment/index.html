Guests can top up. They can't snoop.

An adult has to set the account up with you if you're under 16. After that they're a guest, and you can see exactly what they see.

Most teen cards are built for parents: every purchase pings their phone, and the "kid" gets a pocket-money view of their own money. We think that teaches hiding, not budgeting.

On Kitty, a guest sees your balance and monthly totals. They can send money, set a weekly allowance, and choose a daily spend cap between £10 and £250. They can freeze the card if it's lost.

They can't see individual purchases unless you share a statement, and they can't move money out of your pots. If you share, you choose which month, and it expires after 7 days.

Gambling, alcohol, tobacco, dating and adult merchant codes are blocked for everyone under 18. That's the law and our policy, not a parental setting.

Questions people actually ask.

  • I'm 13. Can I sign up on my own?

    Not quite. Under 16 you need a parent or guardian to verify their ID once, which takes about two minutes. From 16 you can open and close it yourself.

  • Does it work with Apple Pay and Google Pay?

    Yes, from the moment your card is approved, before the plastic arrives. Contactless limit is £100 per tap, same as adult cards.

  • Can I get paid into it for a Saturday job?

    Yes. You get a real sort code and account number, so an employer can pay you by bank transfer. Payslips can be tagged automatically.

  • Will this affect my credit score?

    No. It's a debit account with no overdraft, so there's no credit check and nothing is reported. You can't spend money you don't have.

  • What happens when I turn 18?

    Guests are removed, the spend cap lifts, and you keep the same app, card number and pots. Nothing to move.

  • Someone stole my card. Now what?

    Freeze it in the app in one tap; a guest can too. Disputed payments are investigated within 15 working days and refunded while we look.
