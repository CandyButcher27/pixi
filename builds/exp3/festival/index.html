Soundwith nofloor.

Four nights of experimental electronic music inside a decommissioned heat plant on the Kopli peninsula. Thirty-one sets, three rooms, no headliners.

Modular synthesis, broken club forms, long-duration drone, live-coded rhythm and work that has no genre yet. Every set runs at least fifty minutes.

Why it exists

LOWFORM started in 2021 as a single night in a boiler hall with one PA and forty people. It is still built around the same idea: give difficult music a good room and enough time, and an audience will meet it. We book artists for the set they want to play, not the one that sells, and we keep the capacity low enough that the quietest moment of the weekend can still be heard from the back.

Practical

Getting there
Tram 1 or 2 to Sitsi, then eight minutes on foot along Kopli. Night bus 21N runs every 20 minutes until 06:00.
Access
Step-free entry to all three rooms. Accessible viewing platform in the turbine hall. Companion tickets are free.
Sound
Room A peaks at 102 dB(A). Free earplugs at every bar. Room C is limited to 85 dB(A).
Age
18+. Photo ID at the door, every night.
Phones
Lenses taped on entry. Nothing is filmed, so nothing has to perform for a camera.
Staying
Partner rate at three hostels in Kalamaja, all within walking distance. Codes arrive with your pass.
