SIGNAL/DECAY

Three nights of music made by machines that are slowly falling apart, and by the people who refuse to fix them.

When
19 — 21 March 2027, 20:00 until the pumps stop
Where
Gemaal Zuid, a decommissioned pumping station, Rotterdam
Who
41 artists, 3 rooms, 1,800 people per night
Get a weekend pass — €145 Read the lineup

We book sound that is not finished. Feedback patches left running for six hours. Tape loops recorded until the oxide flakes. Modular systems handed a seed and no instructions. If you know exactly what the set will sound like, it is not on this bill.

Every performance is unrepeatable. Nothing is recorded, streamed or archived. Phones go in a pouch at the door.

The building pumped the Maas polder dry for 92 years. Its turbine hall still hums at 50 Hz, and we tune the weekend to it.

04Three rooms, three acoustics

Where it happens

Room A

Turbine Hall

A 38-metre cathedral of riveted steel with a 9-second reverb tail. Headline sets, quadraphonic rig, the original pumps left in place as acoustic baffles.

  • Capacity1,200
  • SystemQuad, 48 kW
  • RT609.1 s
Room B

Sluice

The long, low concrete channel where the water used to leave. Dry, loud and close. Club sets, rhythm that breaks in interesting places.

  • Capacity400
  • SystemStereo, 24 kW
  • RT600.6 s
Room C

Reservoir

An empty holding tank with a flooded floor you walk across on boards. Durational and seated work, long-form drone, pieces that start before you arrive.

  • Capacity200
  • System16-ch ambisonic
  • RT604.3 s

06Practical

Before you come

Getting there
Tram 2 to Gemaal Zuid, then 400 m along the dyke. Night buses every 20 minutes until 07:00. Bike parking for 900 inside the old coal yard.
Phones
Sealed in a pouch at the door, opened at the exit. Emergency phone points in every room. No photography, no recording, no exceptions for artists either.
Hearing
The Turbine Hall peaks around 103 dB. Free moulded earplugs at the cloakroom; quiet room behind the Reservoir.
Access
Step-free routes to all three rooms, a raised viewing platform in the Turbine Hall, and seated tickets for every Reservoir piece. Companions go free.
Weather
March on the Maas is cold. The building is unheated except for 3,000 people. Bring a layer you are happy to lose in the cloakroom.
